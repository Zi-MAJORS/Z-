AI setup (optional)

The app works without an AI key for voice, time, web search and website shortcuts. To enable AI answers, you can add a Gemini API key from Google AI Studio. A key stored in this browser can be exposed if the phone or browser profile is compromised; never publish this app with your personal key.

AI use may have limits or costs depending on your account and model. Check Google AI Studio's current terms before use.

Commands and limitations
  • “Search [topic]” opens Google search results.
  • “Open YouTube / Google / Gmail” opens the website.
  • “What time is it?” gives your phone's current time.
  • General questions use Gemini only if you have configured a key.
  • Speech recognition requires a supported browser, usually Chrome on Android, and internet may be required.
  • For privacy, speech recognition may be processed by your browser's speech service.
